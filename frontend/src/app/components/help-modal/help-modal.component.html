📖 How to Play

Guess the Indian song in 6 attempts! Each attempt unlocks more of the snippet.

1️⃣
Listen to the snippet: Click Play to hear the initial 1-second hook.
2️⃣
Submit your guess: Search by song title, artist, or movie in the search box.
3️⃣
Or Skip: Skipped or incorrect tries unlock more seconds (1s → 2s → 4s → 7s → 11s → 16s).
4️⃣
Multi-Language: Switch between Bollywood (Hindi), Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Malayalam, or Kannada from the top bar!
